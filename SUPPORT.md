# An unsupported handoff

Cocoa Director is released as-is under MIT and archived after verification. There is no hosted service, support inbox, issue triage, pull-request review, roadmap, or maintenance commitment from the original creator.

Fork it, change it, host it, and maintain your own version. You provide and manage your own keys, services, generated content, costs, and production operations. Start with README.md, docs/setup.md and AGENTS.md.
